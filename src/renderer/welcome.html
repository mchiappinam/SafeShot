SafeShot

Privacy-first screenshot tool

SafeShot lives in your system tray. Capture, annotate, and save screenshots instantly. Everything stays on your machine.

Quick tips

🖱 Click and drag to select an area
🎨 Pick tools from the sidebar, color and ⚙ for settings
▦ Use blur to pixelate, or redact to auto-hide text
✋ Move Objects tool lets you reposition annotations
↩ Ctrl+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y to redo
🔔 Notifications after save/copy can be turned off in tray Settings
